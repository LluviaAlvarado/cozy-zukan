# cozy-zukan

Personal simple and cozy pokemon zukan I needed when playing pokemon, specially when the game is in japanese.

## Zukan tab

Shows a list of all pokemon with their english and japanese name.

You can search a pokemon in the search bar by it's english or japanese name. This is specially helpful when playing the game in japanese and you don't know what pokemon is coming out!

### Pokemon screen

When tapping on a pokemon on the list, the pokemon screen will open with more information about the pokemon and it's sprite.

Here you can see the ID of the pokemon and it's types.

Also let's you know the chances of the wild pokemon being a female so you can evaluate if you should just catch the first one that you met or search for a girlypop.

## Types tab

Lastly the types tab shows you the efectiveness of each type when attacking or defending from other types.

Check this when you forget what pokemon you should bring out next in the fight!
